## Vídeos de hoje por inscrição (Observer) — 2026-11-20

- A new list made in the bot took up to 5 minutes (then 1) to reach the TV, and the TV asked all day for a list that changes a few times. Now the bot is the subject and the TV an observer, over Server-Sent Events on the same `video_feed_url`.
- TV (`web/data.js` `Videos`): `connect()` opens an `EventSource` when the launcher starts, becomes visible or is reopened; each `feed` event replaces the feed (same validation, same localStorage copy, same today-only rule). `disconnect()` closes it when the launcher is hidden. A dropped connection is retried by the browser (the server's `retry: 10000`); a refused stream (CLOSED, e.g. an older bot) gets one JSON GET and a new subscription after 1, 2, 4… up to 30 minutes. Without `EventSource` (Tizen 2.x), the old JSON read when shown and every 5 minutes. The 30 s tick (`check()`) only compares the date, without the network.
- Bot (`bot-indicacoes-youtube` commit `ab52cd4`, `src/tvfeed.js`): a `GET /tv.json` with `Accept: text/event-stream` subscribes (max 16), receives the current feed at once and then only on `TvFeedServer#publish()`, which `Bot` calls after `addBatch` (list delivered) and `swapItem` (video swapped). `: ping` every 15 minutes drops dead sockets; `/health` reports `subscribers`. A plain GET still answers the JSON.
- Tests: `tests/WebVideosTest.js` covers subscribe, events, disconnect, browser retry, refused stream with backoff, and the no-EventSource fallback; the bot's `test/tvfeed.test.js` covers subscribe, publish, plain GET and unsubscribe. End to end (the bot's real server and this real module, Node's EventSource): the new list reached the TV module 6 ms after `publish()`, and hiding left 0 subscribers.
